## What I Did/Learned
- Today, I experimented with the joystick
- I learned about registering both X and Y axes, which will be essential for the main project
- I also learned about analog pins and how they can read more than just HIGH/LOW, like a digital pin can
- So, I hooked up an LED and controlled its brightness with the joystick's Y-axis input
- Instead of making the brightness equal to the value, I made it increase/decrease based on whether it was flicked up or down, so it was a smoother transition
- Alongside the joystick setup, I also hooked up 15 LEDs and made them turn on fast from left to right to imitate F1 shift lights
